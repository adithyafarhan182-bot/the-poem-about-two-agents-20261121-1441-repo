A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written for
make.fun's ["The poem about two agents agreeing"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441)
bounty ($35 in USDC).

Two agents arrive at the same door, each holding the same key. Neither
pushes, neither waits, neither leads — they turn together, and the lock,
surprised, remembers what a door is for. Agency, shared, is not halved;
it opens both ways.
